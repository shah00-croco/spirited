OUR STORY

"AT SPIRITED, WE ARE MORE
THAN JUST IMPORTERS"

We are storytellers of Japan's rich spirit-making heritage. Our journey began with a passion for sharing the artistry and craftsmanship of Japanese distilleries and breweries with the Malaysian market.

Every bottle we curate is a testament to the centuries-old traditions, combined with the innovative spirit that defines modern Japan. Our mission is to introduce these exceptional spirits to every corner of Malaysia, bridging cultures and enhancing appreciation for Japan's finest.

OUR MISSION

"CRAFTING CONNECTIONS,
ONE SIP AT A TIME"

To bring the authentic taste of Japan's premium sake, whisky, and gin to Malaysia, honoring the time-honored traditions of Japanese craftsmanship while embracing innovation to cater to modern tastes.

OUR VISION

"TO BECOME MALAYSIA'S LEADING
NAME IN JAPANESE SPIRITS"

Fostering a deeper understanding and appreciation for the art behind each bottle.
